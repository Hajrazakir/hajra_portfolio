Currently Building

Kaamly

A local services marketplace that connects customers with verified, nearby service providers — booking a trusted electrician, tutor, or technician should feel as easy as ordering food.

Role: Full Stack Developer
Timeline: 2026 — Ongoing
Status: In Development

What can be booked

Electrician Plumber AC Technician Painter Carpenter Makeup Artist Tutor Cleaner Mobile & Laptop Repair Photographer

How it works — three roles, one flow

Customer

Book a trusted provider

Search a service, browse nearby providers with ratings and prices, pick an available slot, chat, and leave a review once the job's done.

Provider

Manage the work

Build a profile, list services and pricing, set availability, accept or decline bookings, and track earnings on a personal dashboard.

Admin

Keep the platform trustworthy

Verify provider identities, review complaints, monitor bookings and users, and keep an audit trail across the platform.

Built for trust

Identity verification is core, not an afterthought — every provider goes through a CNIC/ID check before earning a Verified badge. The platform is being built with JWT authentication, role-based access control, password hashing, secure APIs, and admin audit logs, alongside reporting and review-manipulation safeguards to protect both sides of every booking.

React Native Node.js Express.js MongoDB JWT Auth Role-Based Access

Role: Backend Developer
Timeline: 2026
Type: Independent Project
01

Automatic Grading

Exam results compute percentage, letter grade, and pass/fail automatically the moment marks are entered.

02

Attendance-Linked Payroll

Teacher salary deductions are calculated straight from real attendance records, not manual entry.

03

Conflict-Safe Scheduling

The timetable engine automatically blocks double-booking a teacher, class, or room in an overlapping slot.

04
